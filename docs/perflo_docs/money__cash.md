# Cash

> Your dollar balance: what it actually is, what protects it, and everything it can do.

Your cash balance is the center of Perflo. Deposits land here, trades settle here, cards draw from here, and payouts leave from here.

## What cash actually is

Cash in Perflo is held as digital dollars: USDC, the dollar stablecoin issued by Circle, sitting in your own self-custodial account. The app shows one clean dollar balance; you do not need to think about the token or the network unless you want to. If you do want to, the token and chain are documented on [Networks and contracts](/reference/networks-and-contracts).

## What cash is not, and what protects it instead

Your balance is **not a bank deposit**, and it is not covered by deposit insurance. What protects it is different, and verifiable:

* **It sits in an account only you control.** Perflo cannot move it on its own, and it never sits on a company balance sheet that could fail with the company. See [How Perflo works](/concepts/how-perflo-works).
* **You can verify it independently.** Your balance exists on public infrastructure, so you can confirm exactly what you hold without taking Perflo's word for it.
* **The issuer stands behind the dollars.** USDC is issued by a third party (Circle) that holds reserves against it. That also means your balance carries issuer risk, stated plainly in the note below.

## What you can do with cash

* **Earn on it:** move idle cash into a yield vault. See [Earn](/money/earn).
* **Trade with it:** fund futures margin or prediction positions. See [Trading](/trading/futures).
* **Spend it:** fund a virtual Mastercard. See [Virtual cards](/cards-banking/virtual-cards).
* **Send it:** pay out to bank accounts in more than 45 currencies, or transfer to saved recipients. See [Payouts and transfers](/cards-banking/payouts-and-transfers).

## Receiving cash

You can receive cash three ways: bank transfers into your virtual accounts (including third-party payments from clients), card top-ups, and direct stablecoin deposits to your address. See [Fund your account](/getting-started/fund-your-account).

<Note>
  USDC is issued by a third party (Circle) and carries issuer risk. Perflo displays it as dollars for convenience, and you can always verify exactly what you hold on-chain.
</Note>
